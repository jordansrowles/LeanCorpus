### Fixed

- Fixed a race where segment files could be deleted while still memory-mapped by active searchers. `MMapDirectory` now reference-counts open `IndexInput` instances and defers deletion until the final mapping is released.
- Added retry handling for transient Windows file-lock failures during mapped-file deletion and atomic `segments_N` publication.
- Added permanent commit/search churn regression coverage based on repeated update, commit, refresh, delete and search cycles.

These fixes came from a detailed real-world stability report and reproduction by Apophis ([@FreeApophis](https://github.com/FreeApophis)) in #34. Thanks for sticking with the investigation and providing the workload that exposed the Windows segment-lifecycle races.
